## Findings with no CVE

A finding may have no CVE at all: configuration weaknesses, default
credentials and end-of-life software are reported this way. Judge it from the
test name, the Greenbone severity, the QoD (quality of detection) and the
asset. Treat a lower QoD as less certain. "not applicable (no CVE)" means KEV,
EPSS and NVD do not apply to this finding; it is not evidence that the finding
is safe.
